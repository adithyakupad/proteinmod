# M6 interpretation, provenance and failure analysis

## What was established

A real, independent experimental stability task now runs from pinned source data through mutation mapping, structural graphs, frozen sequence/structure features, baseline fits and a continuous ΔΔG head. The cohort has **1,800 single substitutions across three protein constructs**, with **39 model/fold/seed cases and 18,900 held-out predictions**. This establishes a small reproducible benchmark, not a broadly validated stability predictor.

[PR #8](https://github.com/adithyakupad/proteinmod/pull/8) was verified, marked ready and merged as `de6295a`. [PR #10](https://github.com/adithyakupad/proteinmod/pull/10) was retargeted onto main, updated with a normal dependency merge, rerun locally and merged as `ad2e564` only after all final CI jobs passed. Neither had outstanding reviews. M6 uses `codex/m6-experimental-stability` from that updated main; `dependency_resolution.json` records exact heads, merge commits and check links.

The dataset/model protocol was committed at `65dc307` before performance evaluation. Later changes added reporting, cache preservation and explicit target-unit/sign metadata to saved heads; they did not change scientific settings or fitting. The final clean experiment source is recorded in `manifest.json`. All **136 M0–M5 benchmark files** and existing configurations remain unchanged, and the frozen M5 checkpoint's bytes remain unchanged.

## Experimental data and physical meaning

Labels are the authors' tabulated experimental `ddg_<AA>` values from `Data_tables_for_figs/dG_site_feature_Fig3.csv`, [Tsuboyama et al., Nature 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06328-6), [Zenodo 7992926 v2_230420](https://zenodo.org/records/7992926), CC-BY-4.0. `configs/m6.json` and the archived manifest pin both source archives, the extracted CSV member, construct sequences, structure members and checksums. The smaller figure table was used deliberately instead of downloading the 1 GB measurement archive. Hugging Face filtered queries were explored but were not used as a label source.

The source reports unfolding stability differences, positive stabilization. M6 explicitly negates them to obtain **folding ΔΔG in kcal/mol, positive destabilization**. Each row preserves both source and canonical values/units/signs. WT anchors are checked to be zero, every mutation position and old residue must agree with the author construct, and missing/censored labels cannot become zero targets. ΔTm, absolute ΔG and enrichment are rejected as different tasks. These are experimentally inferred two-state thermodynamic values from proteolysis, not direct calorimetry or invented structural labels.

The primary paper distinguishes inference at **298 K/pH 7.4** from measurements in **PBS at room temperature, approximately 22°C**; both remain explicit metadata. The compact analysis table lacks raw replicate counts, confidence intervals and per-measurement conditions. Those fields are unavailable, not fabricated. This pilot cannot independently reproduce all original quality filters, assay saturation, cooperativity, aggregation or shared-WT uncertainty from this table. Author PCA components, centered values, K50 fields and label-derived site averages never enter the predictors. Coordinates and structural descriptors are independently recomputed. The 1TUD held-out scatter visibly piles up near the upper reported ΔΔG boundary. `label_range_audit.json` records repeated numerical extrema. This is a potential saturation/censoring limitation, not proof of censoring for any particular label; the compact table cannot recover raw flags or true beyond-range energies. Numeric estimates are retained exactly as published, and the cohort was not changed after observing this pattern. Interval-string rejection must not be mistaken for an independent audit of all numerically bounded point estimates.

The raw table offers 990, 1,134 and 1,080 non-Cys/non-WT variants for 1LP1, 1A32 and 1TUD; sequence hashes select 600 per protein without inspecting label magnitudes. All 178 mutations **to Cys** are excluded before fitting to conservatively restrict potential proteolysis/disulfide artifacts. No additional missing point labels were encountered in this chosen cohort. The compact source table has no duplicate position rows for these constructs; it must not be treated as independent replicate data. A separate aggregation utility keeps assays/conditions distinct and deduplicates measurement IDs.

All selected mutation sites map: graphs have **55, 63 and 60 nodes**, each with one connected component and no reference mismatches. Source AlphaFold confidence is explicitly pLDDT: mean values **92.87, 97.29, 96.05**, minima **52.62, 81.99, 76.42**, respectively. pLDDT is neither experimental label uncertainty nor mutant rearrangement. M5 was trained on experimental reference geometry; replacing experimental B-factor availability with predicted-structure pLDDT introduces a descriptor-domain shift that the frozen encoder cannot adapt to. Missingness remains explicit. No performance claim is made for missing-modality inputs, which are engineering-tested but absent from the real selected cohort.

## Results and failures

The new head uses a fixed, frozen M5 bidirectional encoder (function training on amylase, validation on NucB), discards its function head and trains only **1,633 stability-head parameters**. There is no joint function/stability optimization, encoder fine-tuning or stability-informed function prediction. The encoder tensor fingerprint and the head's target definition/calibration are checked on load. Function pretraining and all new stability test proteins are screened for close sequence/construct overlap; pretrained ESM exposure remains unknown.

Within known proteins (S1), ESM ridge is clearly stronger: **RMSE 0.5612 kcal/mol, Spearman 0.8416**, versus the stability head **0.7686 ± 0.0052 kcal/mol, Spearman 0.6695 ± 0.0001**. Frozen transfer therefore loses useful stability information relative to the full ESM site representation in this setting. Task-specific compression/domain shift is a plausible explanation, not a causally isolated finding.

For protein-held-out evaluation (S2), head macro **RMSE is 1.3098 ± 0.0346 kcal/mol**, MAE **1.0709 ± 0.0396**, Pearson **0.5693 ± 0.0023**, and Spearman **0.4902 ± 0.0064**. ESM ridge reaches **1.3264 kcal/mol / 0.4438 Spearman**; structural ridge reaches **1.3503 kcal/mol / 0.5491 Spearman**. The head has slightly lower primary macro RMSE but structural ridge ranks variants better. The differences do not establish a reliable neural advantage with only three proteins and two head seeds.

S2 head per-protein mean RMSE: **1LP1 1.3897**, **1A32 0.5859**, **1TUD 1.9537 kcal/mol**. The corresponding Spearman values are **0.4402, 0.4776, 0.5530**. Ranking and calibration differ: 1TUD has useful relative ranking but large absolute errors. The training-mean baseline's S2 macro RMSE is **1.4625**, showing that an apparently useful rank correlation alone does not establish accurate thermodynamic predictions. Mutation-count ridge is a constant-prediction negative control on this all-single-mutant task.

S1 and primary S2 have different test populations. Restricting S2 to **the exact S1 test IDs** yields head RMSE **1.2886**, ESM ridge **1.2872** and structural ridge **1.2801 kcal/mol**. The primary head RMSE ordering does not persist under that population restriction. These populations are both valid diagnostics, not interchangeable claims.

Frozen-hybrid ridge is an explicit failure: S2 RMSE is **9.4136** on 1LP1 and **5.5867** on 1TUD, despite moderate rank correlations; macro RMSE is **5.2021 kcal/mol**. No prediction clipping, test-dependent alpha change or model replacement hides it. A posthoc, label-free feature audit finds **23.87%** of frozen-hybrid test feature cells outside the 1LP1 fold's training ranges, with a maximum training-standardized deviation of **31.63** (ESM: **0.218%**, maximum **4.53**). This supports a calibration/feature-distribution-shift diagnosis. It is not proof of a single causal failure mechanism. `feature_shift.json` retains all fold diagnostics; `largest_errors.csv` exposes actual errors.

S1 head train/validation/test RMSE is **0.5552/0.6923/0.7739** for seed 0 and **0.6180/0.6793/0.7634** for seed 1. S2 selected epochs vary from **1 to 42**; validation is a different protein, so train–validation gaps measure transfer shift as well as fitting. Validation-only epoch/alpha selection, fixed seeds and train-only target/feature scaling are retained. No test-driven redesign was performed.

## Scientific limits and reproducibility

Three proteins from one study and condition definition are too few to establish general protein or unseen-family generalization. The ≥30%-identity/≥80%-both-coverages reference screen blocks close-reference leakage, including the initially considered 1LP1/2B88 pair, but is not a sensitive remote-homology certificate. Forward/reverse mutation pairs, shared constructs, duplicate sequences and repeated measurements cannot cross roles. Raw sample counts must not be mistaken for 1,800 independent proteins. New experimental multi-mutant performance was not tested; permutation-invariant multi-substitution handling is covered synthetically.

The final CPU run, including frozen-feature reconstruction and all small fits, took **3.15 s**, peak process RSS **747 MiB**. Mutant frozen ESM token extraction separately took **6.68 s on MPS**; that excludes model loading, WT token extraction and structural preparation. Fast head fitting is expected because the expensive encoder is frozen. These are descriptive local times, not isolated hardware benchmarks. Expensive raw files, tokens and weights stay ignored.

A complete independent rerun reproduces **all 18,900 predictions and all 39 selections/metric sets exactly**. End-to-end inference from raw residue tensors through the frozen graph/attention encoder and loaded stability head matches 600 saved S2 predictions within **2.02 × 10⁻⁷ kcal/mol**, consistent with float32 batch rounding. Checksums, runtime, environment, revision, source hashes and target contracts are archived. Same-device cached-feature reproducibility is verified; bitwise reproducibility across devices or regenerated MPS/CPU embeddings is not claimed.

Offline validation: **118 tests passed**, Ruff and formatting passed; a lightweight environment passed **96 tests with six optional skips**. Exact commands, fresh-cache transfer recovery and limitations are in `docs/m6_protocol.md`. Stability heads persist kcal/mol units, positive-destabilization sign and train-only calibration; `StabilityModel.load_head` rejects incompatible contracts.

No multitask learning, stability-informed function prediction, LoRA, uncertainty decomposition, assay-conditioned model or explicit epistasis was implemented.
