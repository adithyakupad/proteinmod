# Technical defense and remaining gates

- Why no external score? Admission is a prerequisite, not an outcome to optimize; four historical similarity flags remain unresolved. New labels cannot decide whether a candidate is independent.
- Are those flags proof of homology? No. They are ambiguous global alignments of short domains, with only3–5 local aligned residues. A profile/fold review is needed; different Pfam assignments alone do not override the inherited gate.
- Has FLIP contamination been established? No. The primary raw abundance column has direct experimental lineage, but the exact FLIP target transform remains unknown. A mixed source archive supports neither conclusion by itself.
- What would an abundance result establish? Transfer across combined family, assay and mutation-burden shifts. It would not establish thermodynamic ΔΔG prediction or isolate family shift alone.
- Were M17 negative findings changed? No. The historical-hash inventory includes every benchmark and configuration at the main baseline. No H1–H5 external verdict is available.
- Can two new families validate a broad mechanistic claim? No. They cannot meet the original three-of-four-family criterion or yield a reliable population-level family confidence interval.
- Is this independent cold reproduction? No. Blind structure reacquisition and local audits were run, but source metadata acquisition still has documented manual/cache dependencies. No new independent operator was involved.
- Release risks? MIT is present. Third-party redistribution remains separately scoped; no tag, no scientific-transfer qualification, no bypass of physical OOD stability safeguards.
